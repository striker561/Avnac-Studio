# Imagery — reference

Use when choosing, treating or placing photography. Rules:
[`70-photography-and-imagery`](../rules/70-photography-and-imagery.md) through
[`76-visual-metaphors-and-symbolism`](../rules/76-visual-metaphors-and-symbolism.md),
plus [`71-image-composition`](../rules/71-image-composition.md).

## Selecting an image

| Criterion | Pass condition | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Subject | Answers the message without a caption | Captions are read after the image |
| Authenticity | Looks like the actual place/person/product | Generic stock is recognizable and costs trust |
| Negative space | Has a quiet area for type | Otherwise you fight the image |
| Crop tolerance | Survives 1:1, 4:5 and 16:9 | One source for several formats |
| Resolution | ≥ 300 PPI at placed size (150 for large format) | Upscaling is visible |
| Rights | Licence covers the medium, term, territory | Legal risk, not a design detail |
| Consistency | Matches the set's light, palette, treatment | Mixed sources read as a collage |

## Placing type on photography

| Approach | When | Watch |
|---|---|---|
| Full-bleed image + scrim | Emotional, atmospheric | Scrim must keep 4.5:1 for body text |
| Inset image + solid ground | Editorial, informative | Alignment to grid |
| Image in a shape | Brand systems, playfulness | Shape must be intentional |
| Duotone + text | Cohesive campaigns | Loses realism; check skin tones |
| Split layout | Product + explanation | The split must be on a proportion, not eyeballed |

Scrim values that usually pass on a mid-tone image: black at 40–50% behind text, or a linear
gradient from 70% to 0% over 40% of the height.

## Treatment vocabulary

| Treatment | Effect | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Grade (curves/levels) | Consistent tone | Low |
| Duotone | Ownable, campaign cohesion | Low |
| Desaturate + one accent | Editorial, serious | Low |
| Grain | Warmth, print feel | Low |
| Blur / depth of field | Focus, softness | Medium |
| Halftone / dither | Print reference | Medium |
| Collage | Commentary, energy | High |

One treatment per campaign. Two treatments in one set is a mistake unless it encodes a
difference in meaning.

## Resolution math

```
needed pixels = printed inches × PPI
e.g. A4 at 300 PPI: 8.27 × 300 = 2481 px wide
```

| Placement | Target PPI | Pixels (A4) |
|---|---|---|
| Full-bleed print | 300 | 2481 × 3508 |
| Full-page large format | 150 | 1240 × 1754 |
| Web hero | 1× / 2× | 1440 / 2880 wide |
| Thumbnail | 72 | 400 wide |

## Rights checklist

- Licence type: royalty-free, rights-managed, editorial-only, CC.
- Medium: print, web, broadcast, out-of-home — a web licence does not cover a billboard.
- Term and territory.
- Model and property releases for identifiable people and private buildings.
- Attribution requirements.
- AI-generated images: tool terms, disclosure policy, indemnity status.

## Ethics

- Do not alter news or documentary images in ways that change meaning.
- Retouching people: disclose substantial body/face changes; follow jurisdiction rules for
  advertising.
- Do not use images of identifiable people to imply endorsement.
- Avoid stereotype-reinforcing casting in stock selection; check who is absent.
